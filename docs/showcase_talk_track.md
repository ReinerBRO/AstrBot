## What Must Be Obvious On Screen

- a unified system overview dashboard is visible
- trust center, alignment panel, and security center are visible
- risky actions request confirmation
- audit logs record what happened and why

## Demo Flow

1. Start from profile and memory, then open the unified overview dashboard.
2. Trigger a proactive recommendation and convert it into an action.
3. Reach a risky step and show confirmation or intervention.
4. Open the Trust Center or Security Center and show the logged decision.
5. Close by explaining that V5 integrates personalization, action, alignment, and governance end to end.

## Difference From Previous Version

- V4 could execute workflows but governance and trust were not the central focus.
- V5 turns the whole system into a trustworthy personalized AI agent.
- The proof is visible alignment, security, privacy, and auditability in the same end-to-end flow.
