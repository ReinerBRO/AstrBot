## Welcome to Persbot

🌟 Thank you for using Persbot!

Persbot is an Agentic AI assistant for personal and group chats, with support for multiple IM platforms and a wide range of built-in features. We hope it brings you an efficient and enjoyable experience. ❤️

Persbot is a **free and open-source software project** protected by the AGPLv3 license. You can find the full source code and related resources on our [**official website**](https://persbot.app) and [**GitHub**](https://github.com/persbotdevs/persbot).
